A companion for crosswords and word puzzles. Cryptora searches word lists using the letters you already know, in English, Dutch, Spanish and French.

SEARCH YOUR WAY
• Pattern: enter known letters and a dot for each unknown letter.
• Anagram: find words using exactly your letters in a different order. Combine an anagram with a pattern.
• More options: filter by a letter sequence, word length and excluded letters.
• If a pattern has no exact match, suggestions may differ by one puzzle letter.

PUZZLE LETTERS
Accents are treated as base letters: cafe finds café. Results retain their original spelling. In Dutch, ij counts as one puzzle letter and stays together in anagrams. In other languages, i and j remain separate letters.

YOUR OWN WORDS
Save names and specialist terms per language. Custom words are automatically included and prioritized in results. Remove words individually; clearing the input keeps your saved vocabulary.

EASY TO USE
Search and clear buttons sit beside the input area. Tap a result to copy it. Choose source-frequency or alphabetical sorting, revisit recent searches and change language, theme and text size.

OFFLINE
No internet connection, account or subscription is needed for word searches. No ads or analytics. History, custom words and preferences stay on your device. Privacy information is available in Settings.

Cryptora searches letters and patterns. It does not interpret cryptic clues or guarantee that every puzzle answer is in its dictionaries.
